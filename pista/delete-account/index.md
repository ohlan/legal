## In the app (takes a few seconds)

1. Open **Pista** and go to **Settings → your account** (the row at the top).
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**.
3. Leave **Also delete my Google Drive backups** on if you want those removed too, then tap **Delete account**.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

## Without the app

Email **[manifest.singularity@gmail.com](mailto:manifest.singularity@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20Pista%20account)** from the Google account you used in Pista, with the subject "Delete my Pista account". We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

## What is deleted

- **Your sign-in record.** Pista (developer: TranscenD) signs you in with Google through Firebase Authentication, which keeps a user record with your email address, name and sign-in times. This record is deleted.
- **Your Google Drive backups**, if you leave that option on: every Pista backup and the small trial record in your Drive's hidden app data. We cannot reach your Drive, so for an email request you remove these yourself: Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → Pista → Delete hidden app data.

## What is kept

- **A one-way hash of your email and your free-trial start date.** Each Google account gets one free trial. We keep this record so that deleting an account and signing in again does not start a second trial. Your email cannot be read back from it. If you want it removed as well, say so in your email and we will delete it.
- **Purchases.** A purchase or subscription belongs to your Google Play account. Deleting your Pista account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions.

## What was never on our servers

Your transactions, budgets, goals, bank messages and receipts are stored only on your phone. Deleting your account does not touch them. To erase them, use **Settings → Wipe local data**, or uninstall the app.

Feedback you sent us and anonymous reports are covered in the [privacy policy](../privacy/); you can ask us to delete feedback you sent by email.
