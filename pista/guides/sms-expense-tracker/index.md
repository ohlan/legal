## From alert to expense

1. **Your bank sends an alert**, for example "Rs 486.00 debited from A/c XX4821 to SWIGGY on 07-11-26".
2. **Pista reads it on your phone** and picks out the amount, the merchant, the date and the account.
3. **It appears in the Inbox** with a suggested category. Swipe right to add it, left to ignore it, or tap to edit.
4. **Pista learns.** Confirm the same merchant twice and its payments are filed automatically from then on.

## What it does not count as spending

Moving money between your own accounts, investments such as SIPs, failed payments, payment requests and one-time passwords are recognised and kept out of your spending total.

## When a message is not understood

Open **Help Pista read**, tap the amount in the message, and Pista builds a rule for that format on your phone. It then reads the other messages from that sender too.

## What you can do with the result

Budgets with a daily "safe to spend" figure, savings goals, upcoming bills and subscriptions, month-to-month comparisons, splitting bills with friends, and reports as CSV or PDF.
