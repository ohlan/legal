## Why people look for an alternative

Walnut made automatic expense tracking from bank SMS popular in India. The app is now called axio and is run by a lending company: its Google Play listing (checked November 2026) offers pay-later credit and personal loans next to the expense tracker.

Pista does one thing: it tracks your money. It has no loans, no credit lines and no ads to sell you.

## What you get with Pista

- **Tracks itself.** UPI, card, NEFT, IMPS and ATM alerts become a list you confirm with a swipe.
- **Learns you.** Confirm a merchant twice and Pista files it automatically from then on.
- **Budgets with a daily "safe to spend" number**, savings goals, recurring bills and home-screen widgets.
- **Split a bill** and see who still owes you.
- **Encrypted backup** to your own Google Drive, and CSV or PDF reports.

## Moving over

Pista scans the bank SMS already on your phone from the start of last month, so you begin with real history. Older records can be imported from a CSV file in Settings.
