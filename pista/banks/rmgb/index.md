## How it works with Rajasthan Marudhara Gramin Bank

When Rajasthan Marudhara Gramin Bank sends you an SMS alert for a payment, Pista reads it on your phone and shows the payment in its Inbox with the amount, the merchant and the date. You swipe right to add it or left to ignore it. Confirm a merchant twice and Pista files it for you from then on.

## What Pista reads from Rajasthan Marudhara Gramin Bank alerts

- UPI payments
- Money received, including salary credits

Pista recognises Rajasthan Marudhara Gramin Bank alerts sent from these SMS sender names: RMGB, RMGBKB, RMGBNK. One-time passwords, promotional messages and personal SMS are never stored.

## Got a Rajasthan Marudhara Gramin Bank message Pista could not read?

Banks change their message formats. If an alert is missed, open **Help Pista read** in the Inbox, tap the amount in the message once, and Pista learns that format on your phone. New formats also reach the app without an update.

## Private by design

- Your SMS are read on your phone and are never uploaded.
- Pista never asks for your Rajasthan Marudhara Gramin Bank net-banking login, card number or UPI PIN.
- No ads and no loan offers.
