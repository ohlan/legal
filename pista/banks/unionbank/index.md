## How it works with Union Bank of India

When Union Bank of India sends you an SMS alert for a payment, Pista reads it on your phone and shows the payment in its Inbox with the amount, the merchant and the date. You swipe right to add it or left to ignore it. Confirm a merchant twice and Pista files it for you from then on.

## What Pista reads from Union Bank of India alerts

- UPI payments
- NEFT and IMPS transfers
- Money received, including salary credits

Pista recognises Union Bank of India alerts sent from these SMS sender names: UBIBNK, UBIIND, UBIONL, UBIPAY, UBOI, UNIONB, UNIONBK. One-time passwords, promotional messages and personal SMS are never stored.

## Got a Union Bank of India message Pista could not read?

Banks change their message formats. If an alert is missed, open **Help Pista read** in the Inbox, tap the amount in the message once, and Pista learns that format on your phone. New formats also reach the app without an update.

## Private by design

- Your SMS are read on your phone and are never uploaded.
- Pista never asks for your Union Bank of India net-banking login, card number or UPI PIN.
- No ads and no loan offers.
