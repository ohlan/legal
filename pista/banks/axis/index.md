## How it works with Axis Bank

When Axis Bank sends you an SMS alert for a payment, Pista reads it on your phone and shows the payment in its Inbox with the amount, the merchant and the date. You swipe right to add it or left to ignore it. Confirm a merchant twice and Pista files it for you from then on.

## What Pista reads from Axis Bank alerts

- UPI payments
- Credit and debit card spends
- NEFT and IMPS transfers
- Money received, including salary credits

Pista recognises Axis Bank alerts sent from these SMS sender names: AXISBK. One-time passwords, promotional messages and personal SMS are never stored.

## Got a Axis Bank message Pista could not read?

Banks change their message formats. If an alert is missed, open **Help Pista read** in the Inbox, tap the amount in the message once, and Pista learns that format on your phone. New formats also reach the app without an update.

## Private by design

- Your SMS are read on your phone and are never uploaded.
- Pista never asks for your Axis Bank net-banking login, card number or UPI PIN.
- No ads and no loan offers.
